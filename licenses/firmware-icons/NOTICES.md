# Firmware identification artwork

M1n1Le55 uses publisher marks only to identify the corresponding firmware or publisher. No endorsement is implied. Project MIT licensing does not relicense these third-party marks.

- SLOOP icon: [isod89 / SLOOP contributors](https://github.com/isod89/sloop-fm1), from commit `ba54ba5f0e7f6e0a1a3499497809e1e6c1ad7e8c`, [`assets/logo/sloop-icon.svg`](https://github.com/isod89/sloop-fm1/blob/ba54ba5f0e7f6e0a1a3499497809e1e6c1ad7e8c/assets/logo/sloop-icon.svg), under the upstream GPL-3.0 terms. See `SLOOP-GPL-3.0.txt`; the original preferred-form SVG is included beside this notice as `sloop.svg` in both source and installation distributions. Runtime rendering preserves the geometry/colors with pixel rounding. Its rendering implementation is available in [main/m1_firmware_icons.c](https://github.com/ZzpsS/M1n1Le55/blob/v0.31-grid-1/main/m1_firmware_icons.c).
- M-VAVE icon: [M-VAVE favicon](https://www.cuvave.com/favicon.ico?v=2), retrieved 2026-10-22. Rights remain with its publisher. No separate MIT grant was located.
- Baud Girl mark: [Baud Girl publisher mark](https://baudgirl.com/static/baudgirl-mark--magenta.svg), retrieved 2026-10-22. Rights remain with its publisher. No separate MIT grant was located. Displayed for FM-1+VA identification.
- Felucca, FoMni, X0X, Jangada, Open, experimental and unknown-package fallback symbols: original M1n1Le55 artwork, MIT. These symbols are not official publisher logos.

The minimal distribution retains compiled artwork data and the SLOOP preferred-form source, but omits redundant publisher downloads and artwork-generation tools. Firmware names and trademarks remain the property of their owners.
